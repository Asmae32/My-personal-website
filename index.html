Horizon Youth Innovators

It is a youth-led community organization for ambitious Moroccan high school student. The goal is to create a community for ambitious Moroccan high school students and to empower, help, connect and mentor ambitious Moroccan high school students through personalized mentoring, various activities, events, workshops, etc that are both fun and educational for it to not feel like a chore but more like an investment of their time, ideas and skills.
